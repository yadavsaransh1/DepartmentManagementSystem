## For Students

### View Attendance Report
1. Navigate to **"Attendance Report"**
2. View your attendance statistics with interactive charts
3. See attendance by subject with percentages

### Features
- **Profile**: View personal and academic information
- **Documents**: Access study materials uploaded by teachers
- **Reports**: Download attendance reports

---

## For Teachers

### Mark Attendance
1. Click **"Mark Attendance"** in sidebar
2. Select subject, date and time
3. For each student, select:  
   Present (✓) | Absent (✗) | Late (⏱) | Leave (🏥)
4. Click **"Submit Attendance"** to save all records

### View Attendance Charts
1. Click **"Attendance Reports"** in sidebar
2. Select subject from dropdown
3. Charts display:
   - **Pie Chart**: Percentage distribution
   - **Bar Chart**: Numerical counts
   - **Statistics Cards**: Exact numbers and percentages

### Manage Documents
1. Click **"Documents"** in sidebar
2. Upload with: Title, Description, Category, Visibility
3. Students can download from Documents section

## FAQ

**Q: How do I view my attendance?**  
A: Click "Attendance Report" in sidebar. Charts update automatically.

**Q: Can I mark multiple students at once?**  
A: Yes! Mark all students' status and click "Submit Attendance" once.

**Q: What file formats work?**  
A: PDF, DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, PPT, Images (JPG, PNG, GIF).

**Q: Can students see others' attendance?**  
A: No, students only see their own records.

**Q: How often do charts update?**  
A: In real-time when attendance is marked.

**Q: Why aren't charts loading?**  
A: Ensure subject has attendance records. Try refreshing (Ctrl+R).

**Q: How do I recover my password?**  (awaited feature)
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login, enter email, follow reset link.

**Q: Can I delete my documents?**  
A: Yes, from your documents page. Admins can delete any document.

**Q: Is data secure?**  
A: Yes, encrypted and role-based access ensures privacy.
